## Write-up approach

For each completed level, create a separate file such as `level-00.md` or `level-01.md` containing:

- Objective and the clue provided by the challenge
- Commands and options used
- What the command/output means
- Mistakes, troubleshooting and useful observations
- The concept learned and a short reflection

## Credential and spoiler policy

- Do not commit Bandit passwords, tokens, or other credentials.
- Explain the method without publishing the password needed to access the next level.
- Keep notes focused on learning; redact sensitive values from terminal output.
- Record only levels actually completed. Do not fill in solutions or progress in advance.
